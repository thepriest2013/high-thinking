High Thinking runs on one idea: the same discipline that compounds a habit compounds a portfolio, and the same distance that clarifies a bad habit clarifies a bad take on the news. Three subjects, one climb — mind, money, world.

A few things to expect here:

- **Personal development** — what actually changes behavior, versus what just feels like progress.
- **Personal finance** — decisions made from first principles, not hot takes.
- **Geopolitics** — musings, not forecasts. Held loosely, argued honestly.

This is written under a pen name, The Priest, on purpose — so the ideas get judged on their own terms, not on who's saying them. Some posts here are drafted with AI assistance for research and structure; the arguments, judgment calls, and edits are mine.
